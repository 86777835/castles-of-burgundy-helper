## 原创插画

两张都由 Codex CLI（`codex exec`，内置 image_gen）生成，2026-11-07。

- `public/img/landscape.jpg`：教程页和规则助手欢迎区的勃艮第乡间风景。原图 1536×1024 PNG，缩到 960 宽存为 JPEG。
- `public/img/avatar.png`：规则助手头像，修道院抄写员。原图 1254×1254，缩到 256。

提示词：

```text
Create one original editorial illustration, wide landscape format approximately 3:2, for a mobile website that helps people set up a tabletop game about 15th-century Burgundy, France. Rolling countryside seen from slightly above: fields and pastures divided like a gentle patchwork of hexagon-ish plots, a winding blue river with a small boat, a small stone castle with a dark wine-red roof, a vineyard on a slope, a modest monastery with a golden-ochre bell tower, sheep in a green pasture. Layered gouache with subtle paper texture, restrained palette: deep wine red, muted gold, limestone grey, sage green, river blue. Calm and sophisticated, spacious composition. The landscape is a real countryside illustration; the patchwork plots are natural fields. No text, no letters, no logos, no game box, no board-game components. Do not imitate any existing board game cover art.
```

```text
Create one original square avatar illustration for a rules-helper chatbot: a friendly medieval monastery scribe with a quill and a small open book, attentive kind expression, simple strong silhouette readable at 40 pixels. Centered bust portrait, with the face clearly visible and the quill and small open book recognizable in a compact composition. Layered gouache with subtle paper texture, calm and sophisticated, restrained palette matching a 15th-century Burgundy countryside editorial illustration: deep wine-red robe hood, muted gold accents, plain limestone-grey background, natural warm face tones. Use broad simple shapes and restrained detail for small-avatar readability. The book pages are blank. No text, no letters, no logos, no board-game components, no board game cover imitation.
```
